Biography

About James

James Mottershead is a mixer, engineer and producer based in the UK. He joined The Chairworks in 2009 as an assistant and soon became the studio’s in-house engineer, a role he held for some years. Along the way he worked with top producers and was guided by the legendary Ken Thomas.

He went freelance in 2016. Over the years he has recorded in many of the UK’s top studios, as well as in the USA and Europe, engineering and mixing top ten and top twenty records for bands such as Slaves, Sea Girls, Paul Heaton and Jacqui Abbott, The Magic Gang and British Sea Power.
